Your three days

Train Monday, Wednesday and Friday, or any three days with a rest day between them. Warm up for 5 minutes with easy cardio, a few arm circles, bodyweight squats and glute bridges, then do 1 or 2 light sets of your first lift.

Push chest, shoulders, triceps
Incline DB press3 x 6-10Low incline, lower for 2 seconds. Gym swap: incline machine or barbell.
Flat DB press3 x 8-10Elbows about 45 degrees from your body. Gym swap: chest press machine.
Seated DB shoulder press3 x 8-10Ribs down, don’t arch.
Lateral raise3 x 12-15Lead with your elbows. Gym swap: cable.
Triceps pushdown3 x 10-15Apartment swap: band pushdown or overhead DB extension.
Pull back, rear delts, biceps
Lat pulldown3 x 8-10Pull your elbows to your hips and pause. Apartment swap: assisted pull-up or bands.
One-arm DB row3 x 8-10 eachPull to your hip, don’t twist. Gym swap: cable row.
Rear-delt fly3 x 12-15Squeeze the back of the shoulder.
Incline DB curl3 x 10-12Stretch fully at the bottom.
Hammer curl3 x 10-12No swinging.
Legs quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves
Goblet squat3 x 8-12Chest up, sit between your hips. Gym swap: leg press.
DB Romanian deadlift3 x 8-10Hips back, flat back, stop at a full hamstring stretch.
Hip thrust3 x 8-12Pause 1 second at the top. Gym swap: machine or barbell.
Walking lunge3 x 10 eachLong step, upright torso.
Calf raise3 x 12-15Pause at the bottom stretch.

How to add weight. Each lift has a rep range. When you hit the top of the range on every set, go heavier next time. For the first two weeks, stop with 2 or 3 reps left in the tank so you can learn the movements. After that, stop 1 or 2 reps short on the big lifts. Rest 2 to 3 minutes between big lifts and about a minute between small ones.

How to track it

Weigh-insMonday, Wednesday and Friday mornings, before food and water. Average the three. Judge the trend over weeks, not days.
PhotosEvery 2 weeks, same lighting, same spot, front, side and back.
MeasurementsEvery 4 weeks: waist, hips, arm, thigh.
Lift logWrite down the weight and reps for every set. Getting stronger is your best sign it’s working.
Daily8,000 to 10,000 steps, 7 to 9 hours of sleep, plenty of water.

Your weight can swing a few pounds with your cycle, salt and sleep. Look at the average, not a single day.
